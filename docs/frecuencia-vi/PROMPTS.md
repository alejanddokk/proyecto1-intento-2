# Prompts y procedencia de los conceptos

Fecha: 2026-11-05. Modo: herramienta integrada `image_gen`, dos llamadas independientes, sin CLI/API externa ni credenciales nuevas. Referencias visuales inspeccionadas y adjuntadas en ambas llamadas:

- `docs/investigacion/capturas/07-ob4-y0.jpg`: materiales y controles; no copiar el diseño industrial de OB–4.
- `docs/investigacion/capturas/01-rockstar-scroll.jpg`: ambiente costero y contexto GTA; no copiar personajes o composición.

## 1 — Señal de Leonida

Archivo: [variante-1-senal-de-leonida.png](visuales/variante-1-senal-de-leonida.png). Original: `exec-aae0db37-649d-4aaa-b7c2-4f4ffcd4646f.png`.

Prompt enviado:

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Create one polished conceptual desktop landing hero for a GTA VI fan-made editorial launch experience named Frecuencia VI, concept title 'Señal de Leonida'. Target composition 1440 x 900, landscape, single screen only, no device bezel or browser chrome. It is a STATIC APPEARANCE PROPOSAL, not an animation proof. Date context November 5 2026; include NO dates, countdown, prices or platform claims. Reference image 1 is only for believable dark radio materials and tactile controls: invent a different radio, DO NOT copy the OB-4 shape, handle, logo, twin speaker layout or industrial design. Reference image 2 is only for the warm coastal GTA atmosphere, DO NOT copy characters, logo artwork or image composition. Design a focused screen with generous negative space, minimal UI, careful hierarchy and typography. Left 38% contains a massive condensed warm-white text 'GTA VI', a smaller line 'La ciudad está en la señal.', and one primary quiet coral button 'Ver tráiler oficial'. Top left small 'FRECUENCIA VI', top right two small text links 'Mundo' and 'Historia'. Bottom left tiny 'Concepto independiente'. Right/lower 62% is an enormous original physically credible 3D analog radio in a dramatic three-quarter view, clean bevels and substantial depth, front and top visible, no random floating components. Wide low graphite chassis, brushed aluminium end caps and fine knurled tuning knob, a long smoked-glass tuning window with restrained amber marks and a thin warm light seam; one rectangular finely perforated speaker grille, one large dial and one smaller physical knob. The radio is the protagonist, large and legible, occupies about 60% of viewport width and 55% of height, not a tiny decorative icon. Grounded low on a subtle dark reflective plane. A warm light ribbon reflected across its smoked glass continues toward a restrained distant coastal horizon with a few palm silhouettes, sparse atmosphere and no busy skyline. Background charcoal plum, amber/coral edge light and cool neutral fill reveal volume; physical specular highlights, realistic roughness and contact shadow. Avoid neon halo, bloom, rainbow gradients, particles and music waves. This is a cinematic GTA VI editorial encounter, NOT a radio store or musical campaign: no Buy, cart, specifications, color swatches, equalizer, song titles, play controls or headphones. Do not add cards, badges or counters. Keep readable text in clean negative space; the object must not cover title or CTA. Use at most two fonts, condensed display and clean humanist sans. Create one independent image, no alternatives, no storyboard or annotations inside the artwork.
```

## 2 — Ciudad en capas

Archivo: [variante-2-ciudad-en-capas.png](visuales/variante-2-ciudad-en-capas.png). Original: `exec-242177b0-621e-46b9-9be9-55ab58a6905a.png`.

Prompt enviado:

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Create ONE polished conceptual desktop landing hero for a GTA VI independent editorial launch experience named Frecuencia VI, concept title 'Ciudad en capas'. Target composition 1440 x 900 landscape, one single screen only. STATIC APPEARANCE PROPOSAL, not animation proof. Current date context Nov 5 2026, include no dates, prices or countdown. The two reference images are material/atmosphere research ONLY: image1 for tactile mechanical radio detail, image2 for coastal warmth and unmistakable GTA identity. Invent a radio distinct from OB-4; do not copy reference product, handle, logos, characters, artwork or layout. This direction must be structurally different from a cinematic dark side-by-side hero: use a warm ivory editorial plane, enormous black condensed typography 'GTA VI' spanning the upper left/top third, a small 'FRECUENCIA VI' masthead above, and a highly physical original radio in a controlled exploded three-quarter OVERHEAD composition across the center and lower half, diagonally oriented. The RADIO IS LARGE, visually occupies at least 65% screen width; volume, perforations, tuning scale and two knurled knobs must be plainly visible. The original radio is a wide low aluminium and ivory-enamel rectangular chassis with a smoked-glass tuning strip and ONE rectangular speaker grille, not twin circular speakers. Show THREE coherent aligned layers only: rear housing, glass tuning fascia, and front grille with knobs mechanically attached to its face. The front is separated slightly forward from the housing; the glass sits between them, all on one convincing shared axis with visible depth, NOT an arbitrary cloud of floating spare parts or a technical parts catalogue. Large dark cast shadows establish layer heights on ivory ground; rough brushed aluminium, dark smoked glass, subtle copper internals. A restrained coral/violet light band passes through the opened radio and continues on the base plane, suggesting radio transforming the environment. No landscape photograph or beach background in this direction. Keep typography and CTAs outside object silhouette and shadows. Near bottom left a single clean sentence 'Una ciudad. Muchas señales.' and one smaller line 'Una exploración visual de Grand Theft Auto VI.' Bottom right primary black button with ivory text 'Ver tráiler oficial'. Top right small text links 'Mundo' and 'Historia'. Bottom margin small 'Concepto independiente'. Use condensed display plus one clean sans, readable UI, careful alignment and generous whitespace. Minimal editorial GTA VI identity, not an electronics shop, audio campaign, exploded-view manual or dashboard. No specs, prices, cards, badges, counters, sound waves, headphones, song titles, player or cart. No arrows, annotations or motion trails. One image, no alternatives, no storyboard, no device bezel or browser UI. It must look like an ambitious art-directed gaming landing with tangible 3D hardware and only essential text.
```
